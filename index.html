Our Story

A Corner Built
on Connection.

Café Connect was born in 2019 from a simple idea — that the best conversations happen over a great cup of coffee. Founders Meera and Arjun Nair opened our doors in Koramangala with nothing but a secondhand espresso machine, a handful of recipes passed down from their grandparents, and an unwavering belief that a café should feel like a second home.

We source our beans directly from small farms in Coorg and Chikmagalur, roasted fresh every week by our in-house roaster. Our kitchen team — all from the local neighbourhood — bakes pastries from scratch each morning before the first customer walks in.

From the mismatched vintage chairs to the hand-painted murals on our walls, every corner of Café Connect is a deliberate choice — because we believe that the details are what make a place worth returning to.
